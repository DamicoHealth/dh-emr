Guides

DH EMR is two separate products. Each has its own set of guides. Pick the product you use.

Field

DH EMR Field

Offline paper-chart entry after clinic. No accounts: the device is the identity. One device on its own, or several devices sharing one cloud key. Also the backup app for a Clinic organization when its backend is down.

  • Getting started: set up a device with the setup wizard, enter your first visit, take your first backup
  • User guide: day-to-day work on a device
  • Admin guide: for whoever runs the organization and changes the forms, lists and formulary
  • Cloud sync setup: for the admin standing up the organization's own cloud project so several devices can sync

Clinic

DH EMR Clinic

A live clinic with internet all day. Staff sign in with their own accounts and roles: Reception, Triage, Provider, Lab and Pharmacy, plus administrators. A patient-flow board shows who is waiting, who is being seen and who is done, and every device sees a change within seconds.

  • Setting up DH EMR Clinic: for the organization lead, from a new cloud project and the first administrator to the join link for the staff devices
  • User guide: day-to-day work by role: signing in, the board, the Lab and Pharmacy screens, the visit form as each role sees it
  • Admin guide: the Staff screen, the per-section Roles grid, and the organization's forms, lists, formulary and lab tests

Which product do I use?

  • You enter visits from paper charts after the clinic day, often with no internet, and nobody signs in: that is Field.
  • Your staff sign in with their own accounts and use the app during the visit, with internet all day: that is Clinic.
  • The two products do not share records, and there is no switch between them. A Clinic organization keeps Field as its backup app for days when its backend is down.

Not ready to set up a real device? The demos are fictional clinics where you can try things safely.

DH EMR is not a certified EHR and is not HIPAA-compliant. It is intended for global-health use outside the US.
