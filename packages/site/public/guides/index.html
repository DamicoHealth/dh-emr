Guides

The guides are being rewritten for the new DH EMR app and will ship with it. Planned topics:

  • Getting started: the setup wizard, choosing clinic or field mode
  • Customizing your clinic: visit templates, formulary, lab panel, sites and lists
  • Syncing with your own Supabase project
  • Working offline in field mode: device identity, patient numbers, sync when there is signal
  • Backups, exports, and keeping your records yours

Until they land, the demo is the fastest way to see how the app works.
