Demos

Try DH EMR in your browser

DH EMR is two separate products, and each has its own demo. Both run in your browser with fictional patients. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Field - live now

Try the Field demo

DH EMR Field is for entering paper charts after clinic. It works with no internet and has no accounts: the device is the identity.

  • A fictional clinic with sample patients, already entered.
  • Everything stays in this browser and never syncs anywhere. Cloud sync is turned off in the demo.
  • Try the whole app: enter a visit, open a patient's chart, change the form templates, clinic lists, formulary and lab tests under Settings, and look at Analytics.
  • What you add stays in this browser until you press Reset Demo. Reset Demo removes anything you added or changed and brings the original sample patients back.

Clinic - live now

Try the Clinic demo

DH EMR Clinic is for a clinic with internet all day: staff accounts with roles, and a live board of who is waiting, who is being seen and who is done.

  • A fictional clinic day already in progress, with patients waiting at check-in, in triage, with the provider, waiting on lab results and at the pharmacy.
  • A side panel, "You are simulating", where you pick the role you want to try. Reception, Triage, Provider, Lab, Pharmacy and Admin each land on their own workspace.
  • Simulated colleagues keep working while you watch: every half minute or so someone checks a patient in, moves one to the next station, enters a result or dispenses a prescription, and the board moves. Press Act now to skip the wait, or Pause activity to stop it.
  • Everything stays in this browser and never syncs anywhere. There is no real clinic cloud behind it, so Cloud sync and Invite a device are turned off in the demo. Reset demo brings the original clinic day back.

Good to know

  • The demos are for learning the app and for showing it to your team. Never enter real patient information into a demo.
  • A demo never touches the records of a real DH EMR installed on the same device. Each demo keeps its own separate storage.
  • Ready for real use? Read the guides first.

DH EMR is not a certified EHR and is not HIPAA-compliant. It is intended for global-health use outside the US.
