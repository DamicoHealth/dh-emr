The two products

Connected

DH EMR Clinic

For a clinic with internet all day.

  1. Check-in
  2. Triage
  3. Provider
  4. Lab
  5. Pharmacy
  6. Done
  • Staff accounts: Reception, Triage, Provider, Lab, Pharmacy, Admin
  • Each role lands on its own queue, the Board one tap away
  • Live board, every device updated within seconds
  • Staff join by link or QR code
  • Needs internet all day

Offline

DH EMR Field

For paper charts typed in after clinic.

  1. Paper chart
  2. Typed in after clinic
  3. Backup file or your cloud
  • Works fully offline
  • No accounts: the device is the identity
  • Optional sync to your own cloud, shared key
  • Backup and restore from Settings
  • Also the backup app for a Clinic organization

Good to know

  • Field visits do not import into Clinic.
  • Both apps can live on one device.
  • Internet down? Chart on paper, enter in Field.

In both apps

Your clinic, not ours

Set it up once in Settings and every device follows. No code, no developer.

Patients

  • Patient number from name and date of birth
  • Returning patients found and carried forward
  • Search by name, number, concern or diagnosis

Visit form

  • Your sections and your own questions
  • Several forms, each visit filed under one
  • Diagnosis quick-picks, one tap

Labs

  • Your lab tests, your reference ranges
  • Positive/negative or numeric results
  • Results labelled against your ranges, positives in red

Medications

  • Your formulary with doses and units
  • Dispensing quantity worked out as you prescribe
  • Prescription presets, one tap

Reports

  • Analytics on the device
  • Visits over time, top diagnoses, top medications
  • Data-quality check before a donor report

Sites and lists

  • Site names, clinicians, complaints, procedures, referral destinations
  • Patient numbers generated on the device, no server needed
  • Changed on an admin device, every device follows on its next sync

Your data

Records belong to the organization

  • Records live on your devices
  • Sync, if you want it, to a Supabase project you own
  • Damico Health hosts nothing and sees nothing
  • No account with us, no subscription
  • One full backup file, any time
