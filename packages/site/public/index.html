DH EMR by Damico Health

A free, offline-first EMR for global-health clinics

One app, two modes: a connected clinic with named users and a live patient-flow board, or a field team that works fully offline and syncs when there is signal. Your forms, your formulary, your lab panel, your sites - all of it yours, without touching code.

Try the demo Launch the app

One app, two modes

Pick the mode that matches how your organization works. Same app, same records, same customization either way.

Clinic mode

A connected clinic

For a clinic with connectivity and a team.

  • Named users with roles
  • Realtime sync across devices
  • A live patient-flow board: who is waiting, who is being seen, who is done
Field mode

Offline in the field

For mobile teams and sites where connectivity is a rumor.

  • Offline-first: every feature works with no connection at all
  • Each device has its own identity
  • Sync whenever there is signal - hours or weeks later

Your clinic, not ours

Everything a clinic actually argues about is configuration, not code. Set it up once in Settings and every device follows.

Your forms

Visit templates built from the fields your visits actually need - vitals, history, exam, plan, whatever your workflow is.

Your formulary

Your medication list, with dispensing quantities calculated for you at the point of care.

Your lab panel

Your tests, your reference ranges, flagged the way your clinicians expect.

Your sites

Multiple sites and your own dropdown lists. Patient numbers are generated on the device, per site, with no server needed.

Records belong to the organization

Your records live on your devices, and optionally in your own Supabase project that you control. Damico Health hosts nothing and sees nothing: no central server, no account with us, no subscription. Free means free.

DH EMR is built and maintained by Alec Damico, an emergency medicine physician, through the nonprofit Damico Health. Guides for setup, customization, and syncing are being rewritten for the new app - see Guides for what is coming.

DH EMR is not a certified EHR and is not HIPAA-compliant. It is intended for global-health use outside the US.
