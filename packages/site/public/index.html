DH EMR by Damico Health

Free clinic records software for global health

Two products, one shared core. DH EMR Clinic runs a connected clinic with staff accounts and a live patient-flow board. DH EMR Field enters paper charts after clinic, offline, with no accounts at all. Both are free, and your records stay yours.

The two products

Connected

DH EMR Clinic

For a clinic with internet all day and a team that works from the same records at the same time.

  • Staff accounts with roles: Reception, Triage, Provider, Lab, Pharmacy and Admin.
  • A live patient-flow board: who is waiting, who is being seen, who is done.
  • Every device sees the same visit within seconds of a change.
  • New devices join by opening a link or scanning a QR code from the admin.
  • Needs a constant internet connection.

Offline

DH EMR Field

For outreach teams that chart on paper during clinic and enter the visits afterwards, with little or no signal.

  • Works fully offline. Nothing needs a connection.
  • No accounts and no sign-in. The device is the identity.
  • Use one device on its own, or connect several devices to one cloud project with a shared key so records sync whenever there is signal.
  • Backup and restore from Settings, so a clinic day is never lost.
  • Also the backup app for a Clinic organization when its backend is down.

Which one is for us?

Pick by how your clinic day actually runs. There is no switching between the two products, and records do not move from one to the other.

Choose Clinic when

  • Internet is on all day, every day.
  • The record is open while the patient is in front of you.
  • More than one person works on the same visit on the same day: reception, triage, the provider, the lab, the pharmacy.
  • You want to see who is waiting from any device in the building.
  • Each staff member should sign in as themselves.

Choose Field when

  • Visits are written on paper charts and typed in after clinic.
  • Signal is absent, slow or occasional.
  • One device, or a few, and nobody wants to manage accounts.
  • Your team moves between sites.
  • You already run Clinic and need a way to keep working when the backend is down.

Honest limits

  • Records entered in Field stay in Field. There is no import into Clinic today.
  • Clinic is built for a constant connection. When the backend is down, the team charts on paper and enters the visits in Field.
  • Both apps can be installed on the same device. They keep separate records.
  • Clinic roles are a workflow layer, not a security wall. Any approved staff account can read the organization's records.

Your clinic, not ours

Both products share the same customization. Set it up once in Settings and every device in the organization follows. No code, no developer.

Your forms

Form templates built from the sections your visits actually need: vitals, history, exam, plan, or whatever your workflow is.

Your formulary

Your medication list with doses and units. The quantity to dispense is worked out on the visit form as the prescription is written.

Your lab panel

Your lab tests and your reference ranges, so results are flagged the way your clinicians expect.

Your sites and lists

Site names, clinicians, complaints, procedures and referral destinations. Patient numbers are generated on the device, with no server needed.

Your quick-picks

Diagnosis quick-picks and prescription presets for the things you see every day, applied with one tap.

Your reports

Analytics on the device: visits over time, top diagnoses, top medications, visits by site and provider, and a data-quality check before a donor report.

Records belong to the organization

Your records live on your devices and, if you choose, in your own Supabase project that you control. Damico Health hosts nothing and sees nothing: no central server, no account with us, no subscription. Free means free.

DH EMR is built and maintained by Alec Damico, an emergency medicine physician, through the nonprofit Damico Health. The guides cover setup, day-to-day use and administration for each product.

DH EMR is not a certified EHR and is not HIPAA-compliant. It is intended for global-health use outside the US.
